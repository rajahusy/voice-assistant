[DOC-001] COLLEGE IDENTITY AND OVERVIEW
Source: https://aitckm.edu.in/about-us/
Keywords: AIT, college, full name, history, established, Chikkamagaluru, engineering institute

Q: What is AIT Chikkamagaluru?
A: AIT stands for Adichunchanagiri Institute of Technology, an engineering and management educational institution located in Chikkamagaluru, Karnataka.
Q: When was AIT established?
A: It was established in 1980, with the blessings of Sri Sri Sri Dr. Balagangadharanatha Mahaswamiji.
Q: Which university is AIT affiliated to?
A: Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi.
Q: Is AIT approved by AICTE?
A: Yes. The official institutional overview says it is approved by AICTE, New Delhi.
Q: What is AIT's NAAC grade?
A: The official website states it has NAAC 'A' grade accreditation. It also reports NBA accreditation, including accreditation twice as described on the overview page. Accreditation applicability, cycle and expiry should be verified on the current official documents.
Q: How large is the campus?
A: The institutional overview describes a campus of approximately 65 acres.
